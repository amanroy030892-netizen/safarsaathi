Privacy Policy & Data Protection Guidelines • Peer-to-Peer Carpooling Network

Last Updated: October 2026 • Version 1.0

Welcome to SafarSaathi ("we," "our," or "us"). SafarSaathi is a community-driven peer-to-peer carpooling platform designed to facilitate convenient, economical, and eco-friendly intercity travel across Assam and North-East India (including Guwahati, Shillong, Jorhat, Nagaon, Tezpur, Dibrugarh, Silchar, and neighboring regions).

Your privacy and the security of your personal data are extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your personal information when using our web application, Progressive Web Application (PWA), and mobile applications.

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable ride-sharing services and ensure passenger and driver safety, we collect minimal and necessary information:

  • Contact Information: Your active phone number (used for WhatsApp authentication, ride coordination, and booking status updates) and your full name.
  • Driver & Vehicle Information: For drivers posting rides: vehicle make/model, license plate number, total available passenger seats, travel route (origin and destination), departure date and time, and UPI ID or QR code for receiving direct passenger fares.
  • Passenger & Booking Information: Number of seats reserved, chosen route, boarding and dropping landmarks, and digital boarding pass credentials.
  • Payment Reference / UTR Details: When drivers pay the ₹100 platform listing fee or passengers transfer fare directly to drivers via UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, etc.), we collect the 12-digit transaction UTR number to prevent fraud and confirm authentic transfers. We do not collect or store credit card numbers, debit card PINs, or net banking passwords.
  • Device & Usage Data: Basic device identifiers, browser types, and IP addresses to maintain session security and prevent fraudulent multiple bookings.

🛡️ Zero Data Selling Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, phone numbers, or travel history to any third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Connecting Co-Travelers: Displaying available vehicle routes and available seats to prospective passengers.
  • Instant Auto-Hide: As soon as all vacant seats are booked, vehicle listings are automatically removed from public view to protect seat availability and prevent duplicate bookings.
  • Direct Ride Coordination: Providing confirmed passengers and drivers with verified contact details to ensure smooth pickup and travel.
  • Verification & Security: Verifying the 12-digit UTR for platform fee approval by the administration team and issuing 4-digit boarding pass PINs.
  • WhatsApp Notifications: Sending automated ride activation links, booking confirmations, and direct coordination messages.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate our service:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase / Firestore: Secure, encrypted cloud database storage and real-time synchronization.
  • WhatsApp / Meta Platforms: Direct end-to-end encrypted messaging channels for 1-tap administrative verification and passenger-driver communication.
  • National Unified Payments Interface (UPI): Facilitates direct peer-to-peer payments between passengers and drivers, as well as driver platform fee settlements to Jana Small Finance Bank.

4. Data Retention & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption, role-based access control, and secure HTTPS connections across all platforms. Ride data and booking records are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill operational, safety, and accounting requirements, after which records are archived or deleted.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, review, modify, or permanently delete your personal information from SafarSaathi at any time.

  • Requesting Account & Data Deletion: If you wish to delete your account, remove your posted rides, or delete your booking history, you can send an email to support@safarsaathi.com or message our support team on WhatsApp at +91 6901027507 with your registered phone number.
  • All associated records and vehicle listings will be permanently expunged within 48 to 72 hours of receiving your request.

6. Children's Privacy

SafarSaathi is intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a minor has registered an account without guardian consent, please contact us immediately to have the information removed.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational improvements, technological updates, or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
