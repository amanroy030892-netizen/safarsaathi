📌 1. Introduction & Overview

SafarSaathi (सफ़रसाथी) ("we", "our", or "the App") is a peer-to-peer carpooling community platform designed to facilitate voluntary cost-sharing intercity travel across Assam and North-East India (e.g. Guwahati, Shillong, Jorhat, Nagaon, Tezpur, Silchar).

We are firmly committed to user privacy, passenger safety, transparency, and full adherence to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play User Data Policies. This document provides clear, upfront disclosures regarding the categories of personal data we collect, how it is used, how it is safeguarded, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

📱 3. Device Permissions & Rationale

SafarSaathi requests minimal device permissions, strictly limited to core functionality:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively when the driver taps "Open Camera / Scan Plate Photo" in Step 2 of Post Ride to automatically read the car's registration plate, or when capturing Aadhaar document for verification. Photos are processed and compressed on the user's device.
  • Read / Write Storage (Photos/Media): Used when the user voluntarily uploads a receiving UPI QR screenshot (Step 3) or profile photograph.
  • Network & Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to communicate with Google Cloud Firestore and sync live vehicle availability in real time across devices.

Users may revoke permissions at any time via Android System Settings. Revoking camera access will simply require the user to capture document photos via their standard file chooser.

💳 4. Financial Services & Payment Transparency

SafarSaathi adheres strictly to Google Play Financial Services and UPI Guidelines:

  • Peer-to-Peer Cost Sharing: SafarSaathi is not a transport carrier or payment gateway. Passenger fares are cost-sharing contributions transferred directly from the passenger to the driver's own UPI QR code (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM) upon booking.
  • Platform Listing Fee: Drivers pay a flat, transparent ₹100 platform maintenance fee per vehicle listing to Jana Small Finance Bank (`6901027507@nyes`).
  • No Sensitive Banking Credentials: The App never requests, stores, or intercepts UPI PINs, bank account numbers, passwords, OTPs, or credit/debit card numbers. All monetary transfers happen natively inside third-party authorized UPI applications.

🔒 5. Data Security & Encryption

All communications between the App, our servers, and Google Cloud Firestore are protected using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) encryption.

Data stored locally on devices (such as session tokens and recent booking history) is managed securely via browser sandbox storage. All vehicle listings and booking passes are automatically retained for 48 hours to assist with trip completion, after which expired travel records are archived.

🗑️ 6. Account Deletion & Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, every user has the absolute right to request the complete deletion of their account, identity data, and associated vehicle records.

How to request deletion:

  • Send an email to support@safarsaathi.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your 10-digit registered mobile number.
  • Or message our Admin WhatsApp hotline at +91 6901027507.

Upon receipt and identity verification, all personal identifiers, profile photos, Aadhaar images, and ride histories will be permanently and irreversibly purged from our databases within 48 business hours.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

SafarSaathi is intended for adult travelers and drivers who are legally permitted to operate motor vehicles or enter binding carpool arrangements (aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 years of age.
